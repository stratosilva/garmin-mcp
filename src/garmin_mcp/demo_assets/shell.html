THE THINKING BEHIND THE PRODUCT

From a personal problem
to a working decision tool.

01 / FRAME

Choose a useful decision.

“What kind of training makes sense today?” gives the product a clear purpose, beyond displaying all the available metrics.

02 / CONNECT

Make the inputs work together.

Combine wearable signals, gym measurements and manual injury reports. Let people fix missing or inaccurate workout details.

03 / EXPLAIN

Make the reasoning inspectable.

Keep measured values, derived estimates and AI guidance distinct. Show the context behind a suggestion, with the athlete in control.

04 / LEARN

Build, test, refine.

Real use led to improvements in fitness calibration, manual-workout merging and injury history. The next evaluation: whether advice is useful, clear and acted on.
